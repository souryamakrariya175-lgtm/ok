To My Beautiful girl 💌

My dearest Shrishti, ❤️

First of all, congratulations. You have successfully managed to become my favourite notification, my favourite distraction, and the reason I randomly smile at my phone like an idiot.

I honestly don't know how you do it. One moment I'm doing something important, and the next moment I'm thinking about your face, your smile, or some ridiculous thing you said hours ago.

And don't even get me started on your attitude . The way you can annoy me, tease me, act all innocent, and then somehow make me miss you even more? Absolutely unfair.

Unfortunately for me, the judge is also you, the jury is also you, and I'm pretty sure my sentence is going to be a lifetime of loving you.

You know what I want? The little things. Random conversations that turn into hours, laughing over the dumbest jokes, being there when your day isn't going well, and making memories that we'll still laugh about years later.

I want the forehead kisses, the hand-holding, the comfortable silences, the little hugs that somehow make an ordinary day feel special, and those moments when a simple look says everything we can't put into words.

And yes, I fully intend to steal a few kisses too. Obviously, only with your permission, Your Majesty. A royal boyfriend must respect royal boundaries. 😌❤️

Also, imagine our future selves arguing over something completely silly, stealing each other's snacks, and laughing about how dramatic we used to be. And who knows? Maybe one day there'll be tiny little versions of us running around, inheriting your expressions and my terrible jokes. We should probably prepare for maximum chaos. 😂

But jokes aside, I don't want to rush through the beautiful things we still have ahead of us. I want us to grow, learn, support each other, and build our own story one little moment at a time.

You don't have to be perfect for me. You can have your moody days, your quiet days, your dramatic days, and your days when you just want someone to listen. I hope you always feel that you can be yourself around me.

So here's my royal declaration: you are officially my favourite person to tease, my favourite person to miss, and one of the most special people in my world.

And if you ever ask me why I love you, don't expect a short answer. I'd need an entire library, several notebooks, and probably a sequel.

Until then, just remember that somewhere out here is a person who smiles because of you, thinks about you more than he admits, and is making this whole website just to see you smile.

Forever your favourite nuisance, ❤️

Your boy, with all his heart.
